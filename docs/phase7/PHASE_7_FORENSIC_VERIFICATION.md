## 1. Forensic Audit of Numerical Inconsistencies

### 1.1 The CCA Bootstrap Confidence Interval Paradox
In the Phase 7 report, multiple canonical correlations exhibited the anomaly where the point estimate was lower than the lower bound of its reported $95\%$ bootstrap confidence interval:
- Molecular–Structural: Reported $r_1 = 0.5767$, Reported CI: $[0.6230, 0.7415]$
- Molecular–Temporal: Reported $r_1 = 0.4186$, Reported CI: $[0.5398, 0.6522]$
- Structural–Temporal: Reported $r_1 = 0.4660$, Reported CI: $[0.5459, 0.6707]$
- Raw MRI–Temporal: Reported $r_1 = 0.8647$, Reported CI: $[0.9897, 1.0000]$

#### Forensic Root Cause Investigation:
In `scripts/phase7/run_biological_relationship_discovery.py`, lines 102–118:
```python
def bootstrap_cca_ci(X1, X2, n_boots=1000, seed=42):
    for b in range(n_boots):
        idx = rng.choice(n, size=n, replace=True)
        boot_corrs[b] = run_cca_pair(X1[idx], X2[idx], n_components=1)["r1"]
    ci_low = float(np.percentile(boot_corrs, 2.5))
    ci_high = float(np.percentile(boot_corrs, 97.5))
```
* **Mathematical Mechanism**: Canonical Correlation Analysis optimizes projection vectors to maximize in-sample Pearson correlation:
  $$\hat{r}_1 = \max_{\mathbf{u}, \mathbf{v}} \text{Corr}(X_1 \mathbf{u}, X_2 \mathbf{v})$$
  Because $\hat{r}_1$ is bounded above by $1.0$ and is an optimized sample maximum, it is a strictly upwardly biased estimator of population canonical correlation.
* **The Bootstrap Multiplier**: When a bootstrap sample with replacement of size $N=341$ is drawn, only $N \cdot (1 - e^{-1}) \approx 215$ unique observations are sampled, with the remaining $126$ entries being exact duplicates. Fitting $23$ weights on $215$ effective points allows the optimizer to overfit more severely than on the full $341$ unique points.
* **Empirical Verification on Pure Noise**:
  To confirm this mechanism, we executed a simulation on two independent, uncorrelated Gaussian matrices ($N=341, p=23, r_{\text{true}} = 0.0000$):
  - Full sample in-sample point estimate: $\hat{r} = 0.4909$ (pure noise overfitting).
  - Bootstrap in-sample distribution: $\text{mean} = 0.6386$, $2.5\% = \mathbf{0.5904}$, $97.5\% = \mathbf{0.6911}$.
* **Conclusion**: The reported bootstrap confidence intervals were **INCORRECT**. They did not capture the sampling distribution of the population canonical correlation; rather, they captured the distribution of an increasingly overfitted resample statistic.

## 7. Final Strategic Decision for SIH 2026

### Question 1: Does QVBE demonstrate complementary information beyond the tested classical controls?
* **Scientific Verdict**: **NO PROVEN ADVANTAGE OVER PCA**. 
* In out-of-sample canonical correlation analysis, Linear PCA-8 captures higher cross-modal correlation ($r = 0.4119$) than QVBE ($r = 0.2147$). 
* QVBE demonstrates non-linear regularizing capacity and parity with classical Kernel PCA ($r = 0.1885$, $p = 1.0000$), but does not establish superior feature extraction over linear dimensionality reduction on tabular data.

### Question 2: Is there statistically supported molecular–structural alignment?
* **Scientific Verdict**: **YES, BUT PRIMARILY CAPTURED BY CLASSICAL MANIFOLDS**. 
* Molecular biofluids and structural brain morphometry exhibit significant canonical alignment (Linear PCA-8: $p < 0.001$, FDR $q < 0.001$). 
* For QVBE, the permutation test was nominally significant ($p = 0.0300$), but **failed False Discovery Rate correction ($q = 0.0840$)**. Claims of quantum-specific molecular-structural alignment must be qualified.

### Question 3: Are the three clinical subgroups reproducible?
* **Scientific Verdict**: **YES, WITH MODERATE PROGRESSION SEPARATION**. 
* When target leakage is strictly removed, unsupervised K-means clustering on baseline biomarkers and motor velocity reliably identifies three stable clusters ($\text{Mean ARI} = 0.9908$):
  - Slow Progressors: $25.4\%$ 5-year progression rate.
  - Typical Progressors: $48.2\%$ 5-year progression rate.
  - Rapid Progressors: $69.8\%$ 5-year progression rate.
* The original claim of a $13.3\%$ vs $71.8\%$ divergence was inflated by supervised target leakage and must not be presented as a purely baseline discovery.

### Question 4: Does QCDF genuinely improve multimodal integration?
* **Scientific Verdict**: **YES, VIA GATING / MASKING, BUT NOT VIA CONCATENATION**. 
* On full trimodal inputs, gated QCDF achieves AUROC $0.5789$, performing similarly to concatenation ($0.6008$). 
* However, QCDF's true architectural value is **modality availability masking**: when uninformative or missing modalities (such as static MRI) are masked out, QCDF recovers to AUROC **$0.6304 \pm 0.0289$**. QCDF acts as an effective noise filter, not an additive feature amplifier.

### Question 5: Which findings can safely be presented in the SIH demonstration?
The following findings are verified, mathematically sound, and defensible before a technical jury:
1. **The Temporal Jump**: CTCLM modeling of early 1-year clinical trajectories predicts 5-year motor progression with AUROC **$0.6417 \pm 0.0439$**, jumping $+0.160$ over static cross-sectional morphometry ($0.4818$, $p < 0.0001$).
2. **Tau Co-Pathology in Matched Pairs**: In exact-matched pairs with identical baseline UPDRS-III scores, future progressors exhibit significantly elevated CSF $\text{pTau}_{181}$ ($\Delta = +0.358\text{ std}$, $p = 1.96 \times 10^{-5}$).
3. **QCDF Availability Masking**: Adaptive gating successfully prevents performance collapse when neuroimaging is missing, recovering performance to $0.6304$.
4. **Unsupervised Phenotypic Stratification**: SAA-positive patients can be clustered into slow ($25.4\%$) vs rapid ($69.8\%$) progression archetypes using baseline velocity and biofluids.
5. **Phase 4–5 Parity**: QVBE-v2 achieves statistical parity with classical LightGBM on CSF SAA positivity ($0.8633$ vs $0.8615$, $p = 0.0802$), providing complementary non-linear regularization without computational supremacy.

### Question 6: Which findings must remain exploratory?
The following claims must be qualified or withheld from authoritative assertions:
1. **QVBE Supremacy in CCA**: In-sample $r_1 = 0.5767$ cannot be claimed as superior to PCA because out-of-sample CV shows PCA ($r=0.4119$) outperforms QVBE ($r=0.2147$), and QVBE's FDR $q$-value was $0.0840$.
2. **Baseline NfL Stratification in Identical Motor Patients**: Plasma NfL is non-significant in baseline-matched patients ($p = 0.7633$ and $p = 0.9557$). Only tau co-pathology and temporal velocity are significant.
3. **13.3% vs 71.8% Pure Baseline Subgroup Discovery**: This extreme separation was assisted by supervised target leakage; the true unsupervised separation is $25.4\%$ vs $69.8\%$.
