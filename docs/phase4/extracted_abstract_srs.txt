03  |  SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & METHODOLOGY — CONTINUED

Quantum Variational Biomarker Embedding (QVBE)
The quantum engine maps these inputs into a high-dimensional Hilbert space using cyclic U_3 unitaries. Superpositional entanglement (H + Z + CNOT) and a Hierarchical Quantum Attention-Fourier (QAF) layer are used to identify non-linear epistatic synergies that classical linear thresholds can miss.
The QAF layer uses controlled phase-rotation gates to capture global frequency-based structures, supporting identification of broad brain connectivity shifts versus localized lesions.
Optimization & Explainability
The pipeline employs a Two-Stage Convex Warm-Start Optimization before nonconvex refinement to stabilize the learning landscape and reduce barren-plateau trapping. Gradients are computed analytically through the parameter-shift rule.
For clinical trust, NĀḌĪ-Q uses dual-head explainability: intrinsic Q-XAI via von Neumann state entropy and real-time 15 FPS LiteViT-CAM heatmaps to visually localize abnormalities on MRI slices.
